Welcome to Odomate! This Privacy Policy explains how Oleksandr Konovalov ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Odomate mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using Odomate, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Contact Information: - Developer: Oleksandr Konovalov - Email: app@odomate.net - Website: odomate.net

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Account Information: - Email address (for authentication via Supabase) - Password (encrypted and securely stored)

Vehicle Information: - Vehicle make, model, year - VIN number - Odometer readings - Maintenance history and schedules - Notes and custom data you add

Trip Data: - Trip start/end times and locations - Distance traveled - Route information - Trip notes and categories

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Location Data: - GPS coordinates while a trip is being tracked in the background or while in-app navigation is active - Location data is stored locally on your device and synced to Supabase for backup - We do NOT share precise GPS coordinates with third parties except for our mapping provider (Mapbox) when displaying maps, looking up addresses (e.g. when adding a vehicle's location) or calculating routes for turn-by-turn navigation (start point, destination and route coordinates) - Location permission is required for trip tracking and navigation features - When the satellite (GPS) signal is jammed or spoofed, the app may use the approximate network-based location provided by your device's operating system (Wi-Fi and cell towers, via Google Play services on Android or Apple Core Location on iOS) to estimate your position and detect GPS spoofing. This is processed on your device; the app does not collect or upload lists of cell towers or Wi-Fi networks

Device Information: - Device model and manufacturer - Operating system version (Android/iOS) - App version - Device language and region settings

Usage Analytics and Diagnostics: - Screen views and feature usage - App performance metrics and crash reports - Technical identifiers such as user ID, device model, OS version, and app version These data are used solely for application diagnostics, performance monitoring, and improving app stability.

Important: We do NOT collect or store precise GPS coordinates for analytics purposes. Location data is only used for core app functionality (background trip tracking and navigation) and is never shared with analytics services.

2.3 Third-Party Services Integration

OpenAI Integration: - Vehicle make, model, year, and engine specifications (VIN-derived data) - Mileage information - Used ONLY for AI-powered maintenance interval recommendations - We do NOT send: personal information, trip routes, or precise locations to OpenAI

3.3 Service Improvement

  • Analyze app usage patterns to improve user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage Locations

Cloud Storage (Supabase): - User account data, vehicle information, and backups are stored on cloud servers operated by Supabase - Server locations depend on the selected Supabase region and may be located outside your country of residence - Supabase applies appropriate safeguards for international data transfers in accordance with GDPR

Trip Tracking Location Data: - Location data recorded during background trip tracking is stored locally on your device and synced to Supabase as described above - Map rendering, address geocoding and route calculation for navigation are handled by our mapping provider, Mapbox (see Section 5.2)

4.2 Data Security

We protect your data using the following measures:

  • Data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols
  • Data stored in the cloud is encrypted at rest on Supabase servers using industry-standard encryption (AES-256)
  • Data stored locally on your device is protected by the operating system's application sandbox and security mechanisms (iOS/Android)
  • Secure authentication with Supabase Auth
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • No third-party advertising networks with access to your data

4.3 Data Retention

  • Active Users: Your data is retained as long as you maintain an active account
  • Deleted Accounts: All personal data is deleted immediately upon account deletion
  • Trip History: Stored indefinitely unless you manually delete trips
  • Analytics Data: Analytics data is retained for 26 months (Firebase default)
  • Advertising Measurement Data: Data sent to Meta for measuring our ads is retained by Meta under its own privacy policy; we do not keep a separate copy of it

5. Third-Party Services

Odomate integrates with the following third-party services:

5.1 Authentication & Database

Supabase (supabase.com) - Purpose: User authentication, data synchronization, and cloud storage - Data Shared: Email, encrypted password, vehicle data, trip data - Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

5.2 Mapping & Location Services

Mapbox (mapbox.com) - Purpose: Map rendering, address geocoding, route calculation and turn-by-turn navigation (Mapbox Directions API) (e.g. displaying maps and looking up addresses when adding a vehicle or reviewing a tracked trip, and calculating a route when you start navigation) - Data Shared: GPS coordinates and search queries when using map display or address lookup features; start point (current location), destination and intermediate route coordinates when calculating a navigation route - Privacy Policy: https://www.mapbox.com/privacy

5.3 AI Services

OpenAI (openai.com) - Purpose: AI-powered maintenance interval recommendations - Data Shared: Vehicle specifications (make, model, year, engine type), mileage - NOT Shared: Personal information, trip routes, GPS coordinates - Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy

5.4 Analytics & Crash Reporting

Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (firebase.google.com) - Purpose: Application diagnostics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and usage analysis - Data Shared: - User ID (internal account identifier) - Device model and OS version - App version - Screen views and feature usage - Crash logs and performance metrics - These data are used exclusively to diagnose issues, improve app stability, and provide user support. - Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Analytics and crash reporting are enabled by default to ensure application stability, detect errors, and improve the quality of the service.

5.5 Subscriptions and Advertising Measurement

RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) - Purpose: Processing and verifying in-app subscriptions purchased through the App Store or Google Play - Data Shared: Account ID, purchase and subscription history, device identifiers listed below for advertising measurement - Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms, Inc.) - Purpose: Measuring the effectiveness of our ads on Facebook and Instagram (which ad led to an app install, a free trial or a subscription) and optimizing the delivery of those ads - How: the Meta SDK in the app, and subscription events sent by RevenueCat's servers to Meta (Meta Conversions API) - Data Shared: - Advertising identifier: Google Advertising ID on Android; IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking via App Tracking Transparency - Meta's anonymous app identifier and, on iOS, the identifier for vendor (IDFV) - IP address, device model, operating system and app version, language - App install and app launch events - Subscription events: trial start, purchase, renewal (product, price, currency) - A pseudonymous (hashed) account ID - NOT Shared: Your name, email address, GPS coordinates, trips or vehicle data - Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

You can limit advertising measurement by: - Declining the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt on iOS, or turning off tracking for Odomate in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking - Deleting or resetting your advertising ID on Android in Settings → Google → Ads - Managing your ad preferences on Facebook and Instagram: https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences

7. Advertising and Marketing

7.1 Advertising Attribution

We use Meta (the Meta SDK and RevenueCat's integration with the Meta Conversions API) to measure the effectiveness of our ads on Facebook and Instagram. This helps us understand which ads bring users to our app and lead to trials and subscriptions.

If you allow tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt (iOS), or on Android unless you delete your advertising ID: - We share your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID) with Meta - Meta links app installs, trials and subscriptions to its ad campaigns - Meta uses these events to optimize the delivery of our ads

If you do not allow tracking on iOS: the IDFA is not collected or shared. Install and subscription events may still be sent to Meta without it, together with the other data listed in section 5.5, and are used only for aggregated measurement of our campaigns.

This data is shared with: - Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads) - RevenueCat (sends subscription events to Meta on our behalf)

You can withdraw consent at any time through iOS Settings, Android Settings, or by contacting us.
