International Transfers (Art. 44 et seq.)

Processing takes place in data centers in North America (USA), where our main database is hosted, and may also take place in the EU. Appropriate safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses) are applied to ensure an adequate level of protection.

Retention (Art. 5)

  • Account data: retained while the account is active
  • Deleted accounts: server‑side data is deleted immediately upon account deletion
  • Trip history: retained until you manually delete trips
  • Anonymous analytics/crash data: 26 months (Firebase default)
  • Advertising measurement data sent to Meta: retained by Meta under its privacy policy; we do not keep a separate copy

Security (Art. 32)

  • Access controls and audit logging
  • Data minimization

Note on Location Data

Precise location is processed only when automatic trip tracking is enabled or navigation is active. No continuous sharing with third parties for advertising/tracking. While trip tracking or navigation is active, coordinates may be sent to Mapbox for map rendering, geocoding and route calculation. If the satellite (GPS) signal is jammed or spoofed, the app may use the approximate network-based location (Wi-Fi and cell towers) provided by the device's operating system (Google Play services on Android, Apple Core Location on iOS) to estimate position and detect GPS spoofing; this is processed on-device, and the app does not collect or upload lists of cell towers or Wi-Fi networks.
